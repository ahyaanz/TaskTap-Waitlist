About Tasktap

Tasktap was built to solve a simple problem in our local community: residents have everyday tasks they don't have time for, and local teenagers want to work hard and earn their own money.

By bypassing the heavy commission structures of massive gig apps, we ensure that the money stays in the community. You get the help you need, and local youth get the entrepreneurial experience they deserve.
